Indian Number Reader (lakh/crore)

Author: Mukesh Jain, VisionAccess

What it does

NVDA normally reads 1,500,000 as "one million five hundred thousand". This add-on makes NVDA read it the Indian way: "fifteen lakh". It works in every application: Excel, Tally, bank statements, government PDFs, web pages and more.

  • 1,500,000 or 15,00,000 is read as 15 lakh
  • 12,345,678 is read as 1 crore 23 lakh 45678 (one crore twenty three lakh forty five thousand six hundred seventy eight)
  • Rs. 1,523,456.75 is read as Rs. 15 lakh 23456 point 7 5
  • Very large amounts such as 2,500,000,000,000 are read as 2 lakh 50000 crore

Numbers below one lakh are not changed, because they are read the same way in both systems.

Commands

  • NVDA+Alt+N: turn automatic Indian number reading on or off. When on, every number NVDA speaks is converted.
  • NVDA+Alt+I: read the selected text, or the current line or Excel cell, with numbers in lakh and crore. This works even when automatic reading is off.
  • NVDA+Alt+I pressed twice: copy the text to the clipboard with Indian commas, for example 15,00,000, ready to paste into a document or email.

You can change these keys, and assign a key to open the settings, from NVDA menu, Preferences, Input Gestures, category Indian Number Reader.

Settings

NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, Indian Number Reader.

  • Automatically read numbers in lakh and crore: on by default.
  • Also convert numbers written without commas: for example 1500000 as shown in many Excel cells. On by default.
  • Minimum and maximum digits for numbers without commas: default 7 to 9. This keeps 6-digit PIN codes and OTPs, 10-digit mobile numbers, and long account or Aadhaar numbers from being read as lakh or crore. Set the minimum to 6 if you want amounts like 500000 converted automatically too.

Numbers that have commas are always converted, whatever their length. Numbers joined to letters, such as INV1500000, are left as they are.
